# Prevalence of respiratory symptoms

Prevalence of respiratory symptoms in 71 school catchment areas in
Huddersfield, Northern England

## Usage

``` r
huddersfield
```

## Format

A data frame with 71 observations on the following 2 variables.

- `cases`: Prevalence of at least mild conditions

- `total`: Number of questionnaires returned

## Source

Martuzzi M, Elliott P (1996) Empirical Bayes estimation of small area
prevalence of non-rare conditions, Statistics in Medicine 15, 1867–1873,
pp. 1870–1871.

## Examples

``` r
data(huddersfield)
str(huddersfield)
#> 'data.frame':    71 obs. of  2 variables:
#>  $ cases: int  21 22 47 3 8 25 31 26 12 5 ...
#>  $ total: int  49 76 166 14 24 81 92 57 45 17 ...
```
